Privacy Policy — Sticker Maker

Last updated: 16 August 2026

App package: hk.android.free.truesticker.stickermakerforwhatsapp.stickercreater
Publisher: HashtagHK
Contact: admin@hashtaghk.com

This policy explains what the Sticker Maker app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and what choices you have.

1. Photos, camera and the stickers you make

To make a sticker you choose a photo from your device or take one with the camera. The app requests these permissions for that purpose only:

PermissionWhy
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+)Show your gallery so you can pick a photo
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below)Same, on older Android versions
CAMERATake a new photo, only when you open the camera screen
INTERNETAds, crash reporting, analytics, and cloud backup

Editing happens on your device. Cropping, cutting out, adding text and the automatic background removal all run locally. The background-removal model runs on-device through Google Play Services and does not send your photo to a server.

Finished stickers are stored on your device, in the app’s private storage.

2. Cloud backup — the one time your images leave the device

If you use Export / Backup, the app uploads your sticker images to Google Firebase Storage so you can restore them later or move them to another phone. They are stored in a folder linked to your signed-in account.

This only happens when you choose to export. If you never use Export/Backup, your images are not uploaded. Restore downloads those files back to your device.

3. Signing in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address. It is stored on your device and used to identify your backup folder so you can restore your own stickers. We do not use it for marketing.

4. Sharing to WhatsApp and Telegram

When you add a sticker pack to WhatsApp or Telegram, the app hands those stickers to that app at your request. Once shared, the receiving app’s own privacy policy applies.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used — for example, that a sticker was created or a pack was exported. These are counts of actions, not the content of your photos or stickers.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. A crash report includes the error, the device model, and the Android version. It does not include your photos.

6. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID to show relevant ads and to limit repeated ads. You can reset or delete this ID at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device, and you can opt out of ad personalisation there.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Crashlytics, Storage: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Sign-In and Play Services: policies.google.com/privacy

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling the app removes the stickers and settings it stored locally.
  • In the cloud: to delete backups you exported, email admin@hashtaghk.com from the address you signed in with and ask for deletion. We will delete the backup folder for that account.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase’s standard retention periods.

10. Security

Data sent to Firebase travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Backups are stored in a folder scoped to your account. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

12. Contact

Questions or data-deletion requests: admin@hashtaghk.com
